Phase 2 · 09 / 17
Find It, Check It, Fund It

Now we get the deal right.

Find

First, we find opportunities that are actually worth looking at.

  • Off-market lots
  • Deals before they get picked over
  • Opportunities where the numbers have room to work
Check

Then we pressure-test everything, and check what can quietly kill a project before you own it.

  • What are the real build costs? What soft costs are missing?
  • What happens if the project runs long? What's the worst-case version of this deal?
  • Deed restrictions, setbacks, utilities, drainage, and what you're actually allowed to build
Fund

Then we work through the capital.

  • What gets funded, and how does it get funded?
  • How do we get favorable loan terms?
  • What stays in reserve, and how much breathing room do you have if the build runs over?
By the time you commit to a project, you shouldn't be hoping the deal works. You should know why it works.

Caught Early · 13 / 17
Student Examples

Real money saved, before it became a real problem.

$30,000–$40,000 saved

The Sewer Line

Student was under contract on a lot for a duplex. Old house on the property, so everything looked good on paper. Until Adriel checked the sewer map and saw no line running to the house, impossible if a house is standing there. He sent his own plumber to check and found an old concrete line the City of Houston no longer accepts. Had he closed, he'd have had to run a new line from the nearest manhole: $30K–$40K, enough to kill the profit. Thanks to Adriel, he passed before sinking a year and all his capital into a bad deal.

Just under $150,000 saved
In Progress

The Austin Townhome

Student is building a four-unit townhome in Austin. The builder worked on a 50% cost model and quoted $150/sq ft for the vertical build. With Adriel's guidance, he's running it as owner-builder instead, which takes about $20/sq ft off. Four units × 1,800 sq ft = 7,200 sq ft × $20 = just under $150,000 saved on one project.
